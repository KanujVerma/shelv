Using your moisturizer

Stop the lotion that repeatedly stings. Keep the cleanser and sunscreen you already tolerate while testing the replacement.

01

Before full-face use

Use a small test area on the inner arm twice daily for 7–10 days. Use the amount and thickness you would normally apply. Stop if redness, itching or swelling develops.

02

After cleansing

Gently pat your face, leaving it slightly damp. You do not need an extra serum or a second moisturizer for this step.

03

Apply an even layer

With clean hands, gently spread enough to cover your face comfortably. Avoid rubbing hard or getting product in your eyes. Follow the directions on your product’s label.

04

Finish your routine

In the morning, follow with your current sunscreen as directed. In the evening, this is your final moisturizer. Check your cheeks later in the day and notice whether your usual sunscreen or makeup pills. These are the two things we want this change to get right.

Stop if the product irritates your skin. Seek medical advice for concerning or persistent symptoms. A small-area test cannot guarantee that you will tolerate a product.
