## Whole-scope review classification, separate from pagination

reviewClassification is exactly complete|incomplete|unavailable. Its scope is ALL actually authorized WORKING draft candidates matching client scope and assignment/unassigned selection BEFORE applying the needs_review filter. Unauthorized/hidden rows are never counted or advertised as unclassified. Complete means the server established classification for that entire declared scope at the response observation; incomplete means known authorized candidates could not be classified; unavailable means coverage could not be established. Returned-page length or an empty filtered page cannot manufacture completeness or zero work.

All/unassigned can include authorized published/archived draft metadata, but those are not new review tasks. needs_review returns only working drafts with known missing/stale required human review for the current target, independent of working/schema/payment/publish permission. Unknown Source/basis/review facts remain unavailable, not reviewed/zero. Source-dependent facts never trigger a held private-return/Source/photo audit or alternate authority path.
