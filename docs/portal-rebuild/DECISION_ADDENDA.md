## Source consent workflow, 1 November 2026

**Founder decision:** Kanuj selected “Advisor records explicit client confirmation; client can review and withdraw in Account” in this thread. This settles the launch consent workflow for private consultation recordings, transcripts and notes. It supplements D39/D40 without changing the preserved D01-D48 register.

An authorized advisor records the client's actual explicit confirmation against the exact versioned disclosure and purpose. Storage and use for preparing advice remain separate purposes; neither an existing recording, a private reference, a source grant nor service enrollment supplies consent. The recorder and recording time are server-derived; an unknown occurrence date remains unknown. The client must be able to review the recorded purposes/disclosures and withdraw the affected consent from Account.

Withdrawal must be durable and stop affected future operations and pending private returns under current authority. It does not recall bytes already received, erase published advice, or promise immediate physical deletion. Physical disposition follows D39 and proven applicable obligations: never-started draft material uses authorized manual deletion with no abandonment timer; started service uses the confirmed support-end basis and twelve calendar months. An unknown retention/obligation basis cannot be presented as clearance.

The client Account projection must derive the client from the actual session and expose only the information needed to review/withdraw consent. It must not expose raw recordings, private advisor notes, internal evidence references, credentials or a client selector. Consent withdrawal is distinct from raw export/copy requests and account deletion. No training, automatic recording/transcription, secondary-use permission or task-agent raw access is granted by this decision.

**Implementation state:** approved workflow, not an implemented or activated Source endpoint. The reviewed Source15 model remains advisor-private; its task/client action sets are unchanged. A separate compatible client Account read/withdraw seam, versioned disclosure content, actual lifecycle producers, current-authority checks and independent verification are still required. The exact final disclosure words are not approved merely by this workflow choice. No Source upload, actual client communication, Root native-source import or resource grant follows this record.
