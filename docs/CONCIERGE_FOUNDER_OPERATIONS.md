## Prepare and deliver the plan

Review the client's skin in person or on a call, using photos when useful, then discuss the actual routine, goals and budget. Describe this as a personal skin review. Do not imply that the portal performs an automated facial scan or invent scan measurements. The consultation remains human-led.

Save the actual consultation and current routine in the client workspace. Separate private intake/meeting notes from the public consultation summary. Author the full plan, including product identity/size, reasons, comparisons, instructions and sources. Save it, preview the saved version, then publish the intended immutable version. Publication does not send an email or replace the human handoff to the client.

The client can access a plan after both publication and verified service payment. Either may happen first. A first service-only quote can precede publication; payment then remains pending publication, and the first monthly period starts on the first publication date in America/Chicago. Republishing does not renew service. Later quotes use the server's next coverage date. There is no automatic monthly charge; check the actual coverage dates before describing service as active, especially after a lapse.

## Product returns and portal support

Opening a skincare product does not automatically make it nonreturnable. Check the exact retailer, actual seller, item and condition before purchase and again when requested. Current U.S. examples checked 25 October 2026:

- Target's beauty FAQ permits many opened beauty items with a receipt within 90 days; qualifying Circle Card purchases can have 120 days. Target Plus has separate terms, commonly 30 days and unopened condition. Do not generalize the beauty rule to every marketplace item.
- Amazon's official indexed policy describes most eligible returns within 30 days of delivery in original/unused condition. The full help page was not retrievable in this review. Check the actual listing/order; no blanket opened-skincare entitlement was established.
- Walmart generally allows most items within 90 days, with item/seller exceptions and commonly 30 days for marketplace orders. Purchase History supplies the actual latest eligible date. No universal opened-beauty promise was established.

These are research findings, not guaranteed eligibility for a future order. Preserve the order-specific deadline, including any separate return-shipping/drop-off requirement. Do not hard-code delivery plus a generic number of days, or display guaranteed eligibility merely because the date has not passed.

The local candidate implements return support: the client requests help for a recorded purchase; Sammy checks eligibility, explains costs and records the decision; the client sees instructions/status; Sammy records the retailer refund separately from the repayment to the client. Use statuses such as request received, checking eligibility, authorized/declined, awaiting return, retailer reviewing, retailer refund received and client repaid. No automatic retailer integration or movement of funds is implied.

Retain product/variant, retailer, seller/fulfiller, private order/account reference, purchase/invoice/delivery dates, explicit return-by/ship-by dates, checked policy link/date, condition and restrictions, costs, expected/actual refund and client repayment record. Keep account credentials and unnecessary purchase details private. Skin photos are not required for an ordinary product return. Customer mistakes, retailer exceptions and founder ordering mistakes must not be silently treated as the same situation.

The client's service-fee refund and a retailer's product refund are separate processes. Because founders pay the retailer, track the retailer refund to the purchasing account and the distinct repayment to the client. The founder has agreed to initiate the client's product repayment within 48 elapsed hours after retailer funds are received. Do not turn that into a promise that a retailer completes its return review within 48 hours.

The client sees one approved all-in purchase total, who it is purchased from, and a short accurate return condition, with fuller terms available when useful. For example, show the confirmed return deadline and opened/used condition or a known final-sale restriction. Preserve the specific policy and order evidence in the operations record. A somewhat higher price may be reasonable for a more suitable return policy, within the client's approved budget and final quote. Reconfirm a changed total or material change in product, seller or return terms before charging/ordering; selection authority is not permission to alter an approved purchase silently. Do not promise an unopened-only policy covers a product reaction after use.
