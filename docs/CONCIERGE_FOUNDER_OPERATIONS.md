# Shelv founder operations

Owner: Kanuj. Sami can perform the same client-facing service operations with his own authorized advisor account. This runbook describes the human-led concierge service: $100 per month, with products charged separately. The Add client implementation is a verified local candidate pending release; it is not a statement that the new button or admission permission is already live.

## Bring a client into the service

1. Confirm the person's name and exact email during the consultation. In the released founder workspace, use **Clients > Add client**. The separate admission capability is required; edit or publish permission alone is insufficient.
2. Review the result. A new record begins in the premeeting phase with an empty case. An existing active client opens their existing record without renaming or resetting it. Cancelled, revoked or protected accounts require review; Add client does not reactivate them.
3. Adding a record sends no email. Copy the ordinary portal link and share it through the agreed human contact channel. Ask the client to request their sign-in link using that exact email and open it in the browser where they requested it. Do not send a founder-generated authentication link or assume a Dashboard invitation has the same callback behavior.
4. If a reply is interrupted, use **Retry same add**. Do not change the email or manufacture a second account to resolve uncertainty. The durable admission journal preserves the reserved owner and exact canonical name/email payload. A later retry after signing back in rechecks permission and resumes safely.

## Prepare and deliver the plan

Review the client's skin in person or on a call, using photos when useful, then discuss the actual routine, goals and budget. Describe this as a personal skin review. Do not imply that the portal performs an automated facial scan or invent scan measurements. The consultation remains human-led.

Save the actual consultation and current routine in the client workspace. Separate private intake/meeting notes from the public consultation summary. Author the full plan, including product identity/size, reasons, comparisons, instructions and sources. Save it, preview the saved version, then publish the intended immutable version. Publication does not send an email or replace the human handoff to the client.

The client can access a plan after both publication and verified service payment. Either may happen first. A first service-only quote can precede publication; payment then remains pending publication, and the first monthly period starts on the first publication date in America/Chicago. Republishing does not renew service. Later quotes use the server's next coverage date. There is no automatic monthly charge; check the actual coverage dates before describing service as active, especially after a lapse.

## Verify payment and arrange products

Issue the $100 service quote separately from product costs, or use the supported itemized combined quote when appropriate. A product quote must reference approved recommendations from the current publication and the client's confirmed shipping-address version. Confirm final product tax and shipping, including genuine zero amounts. Reference/display prices are not final invoices.

The currently deployed flow uses Zelle. The new manual-payment candidate adds the selected Cash App and Apple Cash destinations; Apple Pay is deferred. In that candidate the client selects an available option saved with the quote, sends its exact total, and marks it as sent. A screenshot or sent claim is unverified. Check the actual receipt in the selected receiving account, recipient and amount outside the portal before confirming. Reject mismatched, duplicate, missing or wrong-recipient claims using the applicable reason. The platform records the authorized confirmation; it does not independently connect to a bank/wallet or move funds. Do not describe user-configured manual instructions as verified provider merchant eligibility.

Verify the client's gross quoted payment from the transaction details. Provider fees may reduce the net payout; record them separately rather than adding an unquoted fee or treating a correct client payment as short. Refund the service fees the client paid, rather than substituting the smaller net payout after a provider fee.

Confirmed product funds create **ready to purchase** bookkeeping. They do not place a vendor order or prove shipment/delivery. Manually confirm the quote-bound address and product/size, place the agreed order, and keep the supplier receipt and tracking in the appropriate private operational record. If the address changed after payment, stop and reconcile it with the client before ordering. Exclude every retained **SYNTHETIC / DO NOT FULFILL** fixture from purchasing; demonstrations never establish real payment or fulfillment.

The client records receipt of the product, then explicitly records the actual date they start using it. Approval, payment and receipt each leave the active routine unchanged. Only **Start** changes the relevant routine slot, retaining the authored instructions associated with the actual publication. Never mark a product started merely to make a demo appear complete.

## Follow up

Agree the first check-in around two weeks and later monthly check-ins, adjusted through the human consultation. The client can send comfort/consistency/changes and optional private photos. Review that report, communicate the next steps, and save private observations separately from any client-visible written reply. Calls and messages remain the main contact channel; a portal reply is a deliberate written update, not a chat thread. The next check-in date is client-visible preparation information, not a calendar booking or automatic reminder.

Plan changes require a new saved and published version. Private notes must not be pasted into public replies, financial records or plan content accidentally. Ongoing service access comes from authoritative coverage, not an advisor's memory of a past confirmation.

The landing page promises text replies within 24 hours. Kanuj and Sami need daily coverage for client messages and new portal reports to meet that commitment. Publishing a portal reply does not send an automatic email or text; use the normal contact channel when the client needs a direct update. Arrange meetings separately from saving their preparation date in the portal.

## Retention claim and refund operations

The canonical initial-pilot account and public framing are in [pilot evidence](PORTAL_PILOT_EVIDENCE.md). Use that four-person story accurately; keep the detailed evidence notes internal. Do not substitute synthetic confirmations for payment records or imply longer-term/current-price retention that the pilot did not measure.

The approved guarantee is: if a client is dissatisfied during their first three months of service, refund the service fees they have actually paid. Product costs remain separate. Do not require photos, adherence thresholds, a particular skincare result or payment of future unpaid months. Use the recorded service start/coverage dates and actual verified service-fee allocations to establish the window and amount. Resolve boundary cases with Kanuj rather than inventing a narrower promise.

Clients request a refund through the usual phone/message channel. Record the client, request date, relevant paid service periods, service-fee allocations, total to return and communication. Reconcile actual funds received; do not treat a synthetic confirmation as money. Privately confirm the client's repayment destination and selected supported method. A business receiving handle is not automatically the client's refund destination. A founder executes and verifies the manual repayment, records its amount/date/reference and confirms the outcome with the client. Preserve the original charge history. The portal does not automatically transfer a refund, reverse charges or cancel service merely because the request was made.

No referral-fee rate has been agreed in the inspected scope. Do not invent a percentage, count hypothetical referral income, or promise a supplier arrangement. Reconcile any eventual arrangement and its client disclosure before using it in live quotes or public claims.
