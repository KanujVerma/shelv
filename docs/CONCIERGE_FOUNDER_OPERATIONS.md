## Product returns and requested portal support

Opening a skincare product does not automatically make it nonreturnable. Check the exact retailer, actual seller, item and condition before purchase and again when requested. Current U.S. examples checked 25 October 2026:

- Target's beauty FAQ permits many opened beauty items with a receipt within 90 days; qualifying Circle Card purchases can have 120 days. Target Plus has separate terms, commonly 30 days and unopened condition. Do not generalize the beauty rule to every marketplace item.
- Amazon's official indexed policy describes most eligible returns within 30 days of delivery in original/unused condition. The full help page was not retrievable in this review. Check the actual listing/order; no blanket opened-skincare entitlement was established.
- Walmart generally allows most items within 90 days, with item/seller exceptions and commonly 30 days for marketplace orders. Purchase History supplies the actual latest eligible date. No universal opened-beauty promise was established.

These are research findings, not guaranteed eligibility for a future order. Preserve the order-specific deadline, including any separate return-shipping/drop-off requirement. Do not hard-code delivery plus a generic number of days, or display guaranteed eligibility merely because the date has not passed.

Requested portal feature, not implemented by this docs change: client requests return help for a recorded purchase; Sammy checks eligibility, explains costs and records the decision; the client sees instructions/status; Sammy records the retailer refund separately from the repayment to the client. Use statuses such as request received, checking eligibility, authorized/declined, awaiting return, retailer reviewing, retailer refund received and client repaid. No automatic retailer integration or movement of funds is implied.

Retain product/variant, retailer, seller/fulfiller, private order/account reference, purchase/invoice/delivery dates, explicit return-by/ship-by dates, checked policy link/date, condition and restrictions, costs, expected/actual refund and client repayment record. Keep account credentials and unnecessary purchase details private. Skin photos are not required for an ordinary product return. Customer mistakes, retailer exceptions and founder ordering mistakes must not be silently treated as the same situation.

The client's service-fee refund and a retailer's product refund are separate processes. Because founders pay the retailer, track the retailer refund to the purchasing account and the distinct repayment to the client. A proposed default is to initiate the client's product repayment within 48 hours after the retailer funds are received; this timing is awaiting founder confirmation, not current approved wording.
